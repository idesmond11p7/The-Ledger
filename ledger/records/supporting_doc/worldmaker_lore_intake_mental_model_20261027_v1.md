# The WorldMaker — Lore Intake, Mental Model & Discussion Baseline

## Purpose and status

This record captures the current working understanding of the lore in The WorldMaker repository. It is meant to support discussion, brainstorming, and later handoffs without making the Lorekeeper or Ledger the authority that decides canon.

**Status:** Working reference. Not a new canon declaration, a replacement for source documents, or a claim that every record seeded into the Observatory is author-established.

**Source repository:** [idesmond11p7/The-WorldMaker](https://github.com/idesmond11p7/The-WorldMaker)

## 1. The central idea I currently understand

The Ur-Worlds / ForeverWorlds setting is trying to make supernatural existence feel like a **layered, rule-bearing ecology**, not a bag of fantasy powers.

The surface can look mythological, occult, monstrous, or game-like, but beneath it the systems have distinct operating principles: ontology determines what something is and what it can interact with; energy structure determines how power is stored or connected; cognition and precognition explain some reality-warping; biological Ki works through a different engine; and strict ontological boundaries stop vast potential from turning every powerful character into an unrestricted wish-granter.

The design's signature tension is:

> Vast metaphysical possibility underneath; specific, costly, readable mechanics at the point of action.

This is an interpretation of recurring design patterns in the source documents, not a claim that every subsystem is complete or reconciled.

## 2. Cosmology and ontology

### 2.1 Three ideas that must not be collapsed

The source distinguishes:

1. **Physical matter states:** ordinary states plus exotic phases or forms of matter.
2. **Metaphysical existence:** nonphysical, noncorporeal, spacetime-blind forms that are not merely physical energy vibrating at a different frequency. Some are described as massless, gravity-untouched, and inaccessible to observation within the ordinary observable universe.
3. **Structural modes of supernatural energy:** six ways energy can be organized or instantiated: Fields, Reserves, Connected Networks, Essence, Realms of Energy, and En-Grafted.

These are different classification axes. A physical phase-state is not automatically an energy-organization mode; a mode is not automatically an ontological tier; and none alone establishes how an entity can affect something outside its own domain.

**Open mechanical gap:** the sources identify a missing bridge between spacetime-blind / zero-mass metaphysical forms and physical interaction. Do not invent a translation mechanism and quietly treat it as established.

### 2.2 The Bio-Manifold and life

The Bio-Manifold is the collection of realms and planes that permit evolutionary life. Omni-Life is broader; evolutionary life is a subset of it. The setting allows physical, quantum, and abstract forms of evolutionary life, while asserting that abstraction makes evolutionary change more complicated.

Earth / the Terra-Realms are not automatically the center of all existence because they are a major human setting. Realm existence does not imply ordinary accessibility, and inter-realm traversal is a distinct problem.

### 2.3 Soul 1 and Soul 2

The lore rejects a single, uniform soul model:

- **Soul 1** is semi-spiritual and temporal. It anchors an entity's linear history and probability-weighted future, and is associated with quantum particles, information particles, temporal crystals, and quantum foam. Its destruction is described as locally catastrophic to temporal structure and can expose a realm to exotic temporal fallout.
- **Soul 2** is the abstract, non-temporal Many-Worlds possibility structure associated with the realities and possibilities linked to an entity. Its destruction is described less as a local physical explosion and more as deletion or re-coding of the entity's possibility of existing across Many-Worlds.

These descriptions have major consequences for death, identity, reincarnation, astral remnants, and cosmic stakes. They do **not**, by themselves, explain Jonah Laurence's transition into Ira Lowe. That mechanism requires direct source evidence.

## 3. Cognition, category races, and reality-warping

The documents use more than one hierarchy, and these must remain separate.

- **Category Race** describes broad kinds of life/cognition and their relationship to emotional architecture and supernatural capability.
- **Cognition / ontological tiers** describe increasing forms of perceptual, astral, spiritual, conceptual, or meta-conceptual reach.
- Neither is a generic character-level power rating, and neither should be silently substituted for the other.

A key operating pattern is that emotional systems act as evolutionary hardware and prioritization algorithms. Fear, threat, social attachment, shame, guilt, and related mechanisms weight which futures a mind simulates and how urgently it simulates them. This is not simply “stronger feelings produce stronger magic.” Negative threats receive disproportionate simulation weight because survival failure has a different cost from missing a reward.

The setting calls the feedback between belief, precognition, simulation, and supernatural phenomena the **Illusion of Myths**. This provides a route by which mortal psychology can participate in reality-warping and the formation of astral ecosystems without reducing every supernatural event to conscious spellcasting.

## 4. MAGIC: a dedicated fuel-and-outcome system

The lore defines MAGIC as **Many-Worlds Alignment via Gap Induction and Collapse**. It uses MWI, superposition, and probability alignment as its fictional mechanism. The documents explicitly frame real physics as inspiration, not proof that the fictional system exists in actual quantum mechanics.

The “Gun System” is the central practical model:

- **Shell / chamber:** the intended outcome. An outcome declaration alone has no power.
- **Bullet / ammunition:** the specific supernatural fuel required for that outcome. Fuels are not interchangeable just because they are all supernatural.
- **Blueprint:** the caster's sufficiently detailed precognitive simulation of how the outcome unfolds.
- **Possibility Resistance:** resistance of the current probable course to the desired result. A naturally likely outcome is cheap to align; an outcome far from the current probability structure becomes exponentially harder and may impose severe neural or temporal costs.

This is deliberately not a generic mana bar. Different fuel reservoirs have distinct functions, physical or metaphysical signatures, and compatibility rules. The structured data includes sixteen cosmic fuel reservoirs, but individual names and effects should be traced to source records before a specific character is assigned one.

The broad word “magic” should not flatten specialized ceremonial frameworks. The documents distinguish universal MAGIC from historically bounded practices such as Hermetic, Solomonic, and Decanic systems.

## 5. Ki: a separate somatic engine

Ki is not simply another spell school. The source defines it as a biological / kinetic constant: living organisms asserting physical territory, structure, and velocity against the environment.

The contrast with MAGIC is intentional:

- MAGIC taxes simulation capacity and is constrained by Possibility Resistance and fuel compatibility.
- Ki taxes biological hardware: muscles, tendons, bone, circulation, marrow, heat handling, and tissue integrity.

The four primal types describe different vectors: destructive momentum, structural cohesion, instinctive / prescient movement, and trophic assimilation or siphoning. The operational compression sequence moves from baseline metabolism through trained tension and atmospheric rupture, then branches into inverted bestial distortion or null-somatic erasure. Phenotypic adaptations add distinct bodily and combat specializations.

The visual direction is physical rather than “generic glowing aura”: pressure distortion, pore-steam, kinetic shock, material changes, vacuum-like cuts, and measurable bodily tolls. Costs and failure states are part of the power fantasy, not optional decoration.

The source also includes cultivation concepts such as Tao / Xian and additional occult systems. Their exact interfaces should be sourced from the relevant full records before designing combinations.

## 6. Cacodictyology, the Nethermost, and infernal ecology

Cacodictyology is the study of connected networks and fields of negative, nonphysical supernatural energy. The Nethermost is not just a generic evil location: it is described as an inverted ontological architecture rooted in the Ur-Worlds.

The taxonomy organizes 144 hierarchies under twelve roots, covering pre-ontological abysses, mathematical and physical violations, chronological fractures, Goetic and pactional systems, collective egregores, chthonic dead realms, astrological inversions, exotic-matter dissolutions, liminal border entities, cognitive siphons, mythic/religious horrors, and metaphysically impossible outcasts.

The underworld is geopolitical as well as metaphysical. The source depicts a multi-front cold war between infernal dynasties and judicial systems, uneasy treaties between underworld courts, contested soul-rights, and dangerous entities that do not recognize the established legal order. The 29 underworld sectors and six inverted beast manifolds are distinct environmental and ecological spaces, not interchangeable “hell levels.”

Demons are associated with adventurous expansion, unpredictability, and ending/ruin rather than simply being sadistic torturers. **Cross-source caution:** a separate recovered project document distinguishes the broad Demon category from Devils as a particular subtype. WorldMaker wording and other repository wording need source-by-source reconciliation before we standardize all demon/Devil terminology. Do not silently erase the distinction.

## 7. Astral ecology

Astral entities are not automatically complete souls. The source describes them as arising from intentional or unintended reality-warping and precognitive simulations, leaving Astral Trails that cluster into entities. Their relationship to spacetime differs from ordinary biological life.

Astral realms can behave like a psychic / telepathic information network while taking the form of mirror dimensions, voids, bridges, transversal routes, cities, nations, or overlays of terrestrial locations. The Illusion of Myths helps explain why their appearances can be culturally exaggerated, horrific, grandiose, or abstract.

The 13 ontological cognition tiers and the 15 astral category groups / 150 archetypes are not the same taxonomy. The count, classification basis, and relationship between each set must be preserved.

## 8. The design philosophy and intended feel

The creative guide points toward:

- Mythological traditions reinterpreted through speculative physics, evolutionary biology, and formal systems.
- Specific mechanics with clear limits, costs, compatibility, and consequences.
- Distinct sensory identities for powers, creatures, and realms.
- Brutal, tactile combat rather than interchangeable aura effects.
- Names that carry conceptual and material information rather than generic fantasy labels.
- Cosmological scale that does not erase local rules or make every conflict meaningless.
- Visual and audio production concepts that could translate into 2D / 3D presentation and game feedback.

A crucial distinction for collaboration: **the lore's in-world atmosphere can be brutal, eerie, and high-concept while our out-of-world discussion remains casual, friendly, playful, and gamey.** Brainstorming should feel like two people bouncing around a cool system: “what happens if we combine these rules?” rather than a formal tribunal. The playful tone must not loosen evidence rules. We can pitch wild ideas freely if we label them as ideas and keep established mechanics intact.

**Important:** structured presence is not proof of canon status. The source data itself labels some records as “Expanded in Studio,” “Seed Name Only (Needs Flesh-Out),” or incomplete. A few sample dossiers and visual / realm details were synthesized to populate the workbench. Preserve the source's status labels and do not elevate generated filler to creator-authored lore.

## 10. Concrete tensions and unresolved questions to preserve

1. **Category numbering:** the taxonomy includes Categories 1–5 and 7, while Category 6 is explicitly marked undocumented in structured data. Do not invent it.
2. **Category Race vs. cognition tiers:** separate taxonomies; the numbering is not interchangeable.
3. **Physical interaction gap:** how spacetime-blind, zero-mass metaphysical forms act on physical matter remains unresolved in the source set.
4. **Soul / death / afterlife transitions:** the general Soul 1 / Soul 2 model does not fully decide the fate of every dead Category 4 being.
5. **Realm traversal:** access between the Bio-Manifold, Underworld Sectors, and Inverted Beast Manifolds requires rules; existence alone does not provide a route.
6. **Demon vs. Devil terminology:** WorldMaker and other recovered records require reconciliation before a unified definition is declared.
7. **System completeness:** named examples, hierarchy rosters, fuel compatibility, Turn Field interactions, and some realm details may be partially expanded rather than fully sourced.
8. **Character application:** no protagonist-specific power, morphology, or reincarnation mechanism should be inferred solely from the general cosmology.

These are productive design questions, not permission to patch the lore with guesses.

## 12. What I should do with this mental model

Use it as a map for discussion and source retrieval, not as a replacement for the underlying lore.

When brainstorming:
1. Start with the cool idea or gameplay consequence.
2. Identify which existing rule it touches.
3. Check whether the rule is established, inferred, or unresolved.
4. Explore the fun implications without quietly changing the base rule.
5. If the idea survives discussion, mark it Proposed and trace the decision before it enters a canonical record.

The goal is a world that feels strange because its rules have consequences, not because every answer is “the magic did it.”
