# Chapter 7 — The Examination: Beat Sheet & Structural Architecture v1

**Chronology Window:** Early March 2005, `9:00 AM – ~5:35 PM` (Kanagawa / Soma)  
**Exam Track:** Returnee / Overseas Transfer & Independent Curriculum (`kikokusei` / `hennyū` special March intake, Applicant `1044`, Desk `44`)  
**Adaptive Style Rule:** No uniform house style. Shift prose density and rhythm by scene function—crisp motor causality in Room 301, wide thermal/acoustic release in the glass connector, clipped fatigue and acoustic vacuum in the East/Science Wing, diffuse decompression in town, and thinned existential stillness at dusk.
