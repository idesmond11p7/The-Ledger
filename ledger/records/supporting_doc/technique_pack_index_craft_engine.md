# Craft Engine — Technique Pack Index & Use Protocol

These packs are working methods for analyzing, designing, testing, and revising prose. Their names are retrieval handles; some are playful project labels, not established academic terms. Source observations, research findings, interpretations, procedures, and open questions must remain distinguishable.

## Packs

- **Wraytracing — Light Mechanics for Prose & Poetry:** make light physically and narratively consequential.
- **The Camera Eye — Focalization & Attention:** control perceptual access, attention, interpretation, and uncertainty.
- **Resonance & Echo — Motifs That Change:** make recurrence alter meaning rather than merely repeat.
- **Subtext & Lacunae — Silence, Shadow Stories & Narrative Gaps:** examine implied meaning and gaps that may remain unresolved.
- **Subtext & Ellipsis — Omitted Language, Time & Meaning:** distinguish omitted words, conversational fragments, skipped intervals, and missing event steps.
- **Rest & Arrest — Stillness & Suspension:** control waiting, pauses, held moments, and release.
- **Juxtaposition & Collage — Meaning by Collision:** create meaning through contrasts between scale, register, time, voice, or fragment.
- **Cadence Engine — Prose Rhythm & Sonic Texture:** shape syntax, stress, sentence motion, repetition, and sound.
- **Lyric Forge — Lyricism, Refrain, Sound & the Turn:** develop lyrical prose and song lyrics through image, sound, lineation, refrain, and emotional turn.
- **Glyphcraft — Typography as Narrative Signal:** use punctuation, lineation, capitalization, and spacing intentionally.
- **Psycholinguistic Switchboard — Reader Inference & Expectation:** examine prediction, memory, coherence, ambiguity, and cognitive load without pretending to control every reader.
- **Kinetic Syntax — Action & Impact:** manage physical sequence, attention narrowing, sonic beats, and fragmentation.

## Use protocol

1. Diagnose the real passage-level task before choosing a pack.
2. Retrieve the source passage and enough surrounding chapter context to preserve voice, chronology, knowledge, and continuity.
3. Identify the mechanism, not just the desired vibe.
4. Apply locally, then compare the result with the intended effect.
5. Check side effects: overwriting, confusion, false causality, excessive emphasis, broken POV, or a technique calling attention to itself.
6. Record useful examples with source links and the limits of the finding.
