# Chapter 9 Receipt: Lorekeeper Fit and Astral Taxonomy

**Status:** Working receipt v1  
**Primary source:** `idesmond11p7/The-Lorekeeper/docs/lore/` files retrieved for this planning pass.

## Established by the retrieved Lorekeeper documents

- Astral Trails can arise from mental precognitive simulations and emotional kickback, then cluster into Astral Entities.
- Astral Entities are ontologically incomplete; a complete soul is Soul 1.
- Astral realms may manifest as mirror dimensions, voids, bridges, transversal routes, cities, nations, or vibrational overlays of terrestrial locations.
- The taxonomy includes **The Chthonic Shade** under Funerary / Graveyard / Chthonic Wraiths.
- It includes **The Intelligence** under Planetary / Astrological Intelligences.
- It includes **The Boundary-Stalker**, **The Crossroads Shadow**, and **The Veil-Tearer** under Liminal / Threshold / Circle Guards.
- It includes **The Raven Messenger** and **The Owl Seer** under Bestial / Totemic / Animal Spirits.
- It includes **The Miasma** under Parasites / Leeches / Energy Siphons.
- Corvus-Gallow is an Inverted Beast Manifold associated with avians. It is not the same taxonomy as the Astral Entity categories.
- The Lorekeeper explicitly lists spatial inter-realm transit for non-Category 7 entities as an unresolved creative question.
- The creative philosophy requires supernatural effects to operate through bounded powers, limits, costs, and lanes.

## Chapter 9 applications

- Ira may cross an astral boundary without recognizing it. This chapter can depict the event, but must not establish a universal transit mechanism.
- Do not name the destination realm unless a more specific Lorekeeper source confirms it.
- Use The Intelligence and The Chthonic Shade as taxonomy-linked presences, but do not claim that their categories inherently mean malicious stalking.
- “Avian Stalker” is not a named archetype in the retrieved taxonomy. Treat it as a descriptive working label only, or replace it after a more specific source is found.
- Do not automatically equate the avian stalker with Corvus-Gallow.
- Do not assume the purple void is The Miasma. The term exists in the taxonomy, but the void's identity is unresolved.
- The author's 1% survival estimate must remain outside Ira's awareness and the first-person narration.

## Evidence boundary

These findings come from repository documents in The-Lorekeeper. They are not a response or approval from a live Lorekeeper model instance. The retrieved documents establish taxonomy and broad metaphysical context, not the exact behavior, danger rating, or geography of the Chapter 9 realm.
