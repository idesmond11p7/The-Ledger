# Chapter 5 Analysis Receipt: Summary and Chronology

Source: [Chapter 5 — A Second Morning](https://github.com/idesmond11p7/The-Ledger/blob/main/ledger/records/chapter/chapter_05_a_second_morning.md)

- Jonah examines the unfamiliar reflection and body, then grieves the loss of his old life, including his mother and Daniel. He cries and falls asleep.
- On waking in daylight, he tests his new body's movement and voice, recognizes that he occupies a very feminine-looking male body, and uses humor to process the shock.
- Documents identify the body as **Ira Lowe**, age **16**. An admission notice says Kuoh Academy's entrance examination is tomorrow.
- He searches the apartment for context, uses a printed map, travels by train, gets lost twice, and eventually reaches Kuoh Academy's gates.
- The closing line is: “Today, I had finally found somewhere to go.”
- Exact date and weekday are not stated. The two sleep/morning transitions make elapsed time somewhat ambiguous.
- Jonah's prior-life age of 17 and Ira's ID age of 16 belong to different identities.
