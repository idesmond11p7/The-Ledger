# Chapter 4 Analysis Receipt: Craft and Motifs

Source: [Chapter 4 — Beyond](https://github.com/idesmond11p7/The-Ledger/blob/main/ledger/records/chapter/chapter_04_beyond.md)

- Recognizable astronomy expands into impossible geometry and then into absence, removing the reader's usual coordinates.
- Bodily sensation disappears, then ordinary sound, touch, wind, grass, and traffic return. Mundane details regain weight after cosmic abstraction.
- Jonah's mother, Daniel, Earth, and ordinary life provide emotional stakes for the perceived cosmic scale.
- Chapter 3's mirror records self-judgment; Chapter 4's mirror reveals an unfamiliar body.
- Keep the sphere and cosmic structures framed as Jonah's perceptions, not confirmed cosmology.
- The mirror confrontation ends the chapter at the first concrete proof that Jonah's former body is gone.
