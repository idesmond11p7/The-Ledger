# Chapter 4 Analysis Receipt: Transition Event Chain

Source: [Chapter 4 — Beyond](https://github.com/idesmond11p7/The-Ledger/blob/main/ledger/records/chapter/chapter_04_beyond.md)

- Consciousness returns without bodily sensation, orientation, or ordinary darkness.
- Jonah perceives stars, nebulae, galaxies, a black hole/accretion disk, and structures whose geometry defeats normal inside/outside and near/far distinctions.
- Time loses meaning. Colored currents carry him toward a pale blue-white sphere he interprets as the observable universe.
- He recognizes Earth and the lives inside it, including his mother and Daniel, and fears separation from them.
- An undefined boundary precedes a transition described as the universe shattering into light.
- Jonah wakes on a grassy hill beside an empty road at night. Ordinary sensory detail returns.
- Closing his eyes briefly produces a visual stutter or tear in reality.
- He discovers an unfamiliar body and face in a nearby, apparently occupied but empty apartment. The chapter ends at the mirror with “What the fuck?”
- These are Jonah's perceptions. The chapter does not objectively explain the cosmology, transfer mechanism, or new identity.
