## 1. Senior Proctor & Younger Assistant (Room 301 & Afternoon Wing)

- **Routine Institutional Machinery:**
  - *Senior Proctor:* Stands square behind the wooden lectern, reading printed regulations in an even, flat cadence with zero vocal emphasis on warnings. Eye-line stays anchored to the printed sheet and his **silver pocket watch**, lifting only long enough to confirm every row has flipped booklets face-down.
  - *Younger Assistant:* Moves half a beat behind on silent rubber soles, fanning collected sheets by the corners against one thumb. Passing Desk 44 during ID verification, he pauses half a beat longer—not out of suspicion or curiosity, simply confirming the blue overseas/independent curriculum (`kikokusei`) placard code (`1044`) against the special notation column on the seating chart before moving cleanly to Desk 45.
- **Applicant Writing Two Seconds Past "Pencils Down" (`Applicant 1038`):**
  - Zero raised voice, zero dramatic glare, zero lecturing.
  - Senior proctor finishes his closing instruction without rushing, walks down the aisle at normal pace, and stops two steps short of the desk—never leaning over the student.
  - **Acoustic Pressure Cue (HDR Silence Drop):** The younger assistant halts mid-collection two rows over and stands completely still. The abrupt drop in paper-shuffling sound pulls the room's peripheral awareness straight to Desk 38.
  - **Stripped-Down Lines:**
    - `"Applicant 1038. Please set your pencil down."`
    - *(If the student stammers an excuse):* `"Your desk has been noted. Keep your hands off the paper until collection is finished."`
  - **What Is Left Unsaid:** One small, tidy pencil mark beside the number on his clipboard before moving to the next row. Never stating whether the mark means a deduction or disqualification hits harder than anger.
