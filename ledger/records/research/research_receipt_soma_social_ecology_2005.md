# Research Receipt — 2005 Elite Private Academy Social Ecology (Pass A Audited)

**Core Operational Constraint:** Preservation does not mean activation. Do not treat this taxonomy as a casting checklist where every scene displays all tiers and outliers. Activate only the 2–3 specific behavioral tells that create real friction in a given scene.

---

## 1. The Generic Majority ("Real-Life NPC" Baseline) & True Status

- **Active Conformity:** In a high-prestige 2005 Tokyo–Kanagawa private academy shaped by exam pressure (*juken*) and reading the room (*kuuki*), blending in is a friction-minimization skill. Most examinees, students, and staff genuinely share interchangeable habits, cram-school stationery, safe polite scripts, and mundane worries.
- **Unglamorous Low-Confidence Shyness:** Real low-confidence withdrawal is not "mysterious" or intimidating; peers read it as low social value and stop factoring the person into the room's trajectory. Tells: inward shoulders, half-beat timing lags, dropping a sentence unfinished when someone louder speaks, busy-hand camouflage (flipping a silent 2005 clamshell phone to check the clock/battery, smoothing an already flat collar, staring at a timetable).
- **Calibrated Extroversion & Sociable Dominance (Hawley, 2003):** Teenage status hierarchies skew toward socially fluent, calibrated extroverts and effortless insiders rather than brooding loners or snarling bullies. Tells: comfortable indoor volume in quiet halls without checking who is annoyed, gaze ownership (looking away on their own timetable rather than flinching), effortless group initiative.

---

## 2. Three-Tier Institutional Stratification (*Naibusei* vs. *Gaibusei*)

1. **Tier 1 — High-Conformity Majority:** Mirrors spacing and posture of those ahead; soft, non-sticky eye-lines anchored to neutral surfaces; polite buffer words (*"Sumimasen"*, *"Chotto..."*); eyes slide smoothly past anyone off-script to avoid awkwardness.
2. **Tier 2 — Fragile-Ego Strivers (External Grinders):** Split between silent cognitive worry in top scorers and visible motor leakage in borderline climbers (rapid pencil clicking, heel bouncing, re-checking desk placard numbers, pressing lead hard enough to snap, fast peripheral checks on neighbors' page turns). Post-exam vocal armor: brittle bravado or preemptive excuses (*"barely slept"*).
3. **Tier 3 — Insulated Insiders & High-Status Pedigree (Culturally Calibrated):**
   - *Verified Mechanism (Kraus & Keltner, 2009):* High-status insiders signal rank through reduced social accommodation and lower dependence on outsiders' goodwill, not theatrical cruelty.
   - *Circulator Cultural Correction (2005 Japan):* Reject Western boardroom rudeness (checking a wristwatch mid-sentence). Elite Japanese insiders weaponize **frictionless politeness (*tatemae*)**: letting an outsider finish, delivering a clean, flat closer (*"Sō desu ka"*), giving a millimeter nod, and turning cleanly back to their own track without inviting a follow-up.

---

## 3. Ira Lowe's Specific Slot: The *Kikokusei* (Returnee) Paradox

- Because Ira sits on the **Overseas Transfer / Independent Curriculum** track (established in Chapter 6), Soma staff and students automatically read him through the *kikokusei* (returnee) lens—neither a standard cram-school grinder nor an internal escalator legacy.
- *Behavioral Consequence:* Returnees occupy a double slot—granted brief leeway/curiosity for off-grid polish, yet quietly policed the instant their timing, deference, or indirectness misses local *kuuki*.

---

## 4. Rare Background Outliers & Gradual Breakdown Mechanics

- **Unpredictable Outliers (Use Sparingly for Contrast):**
  - *Effortlessly Unbothered Mid-Ranker:* Anchored outside the prestige ladder; zero-agenda casualness.
  - *Competence-Shielded Eccentric:* Raw output buys quiet immunity from minor etiquette policing.
  - *Hollowed-Out Former High Achiever:* Visually identical to Tier 1, but emotionally flat and running on mechanical habit.
- **Quiet Breakdown Inside a Prestige Bubble (Gilbert & Allan, 1998; Sturman, 2011):**
  - Breakdown happens via accumulated micro-friction and *arrested flight* (involuntary subordination without exit), never one dramatic speech:
    1. *Accumulated Micro-Friction:* Unsaved seats, conversations ending one beat faster when they approach.
    2. *Preemptive Shrinkage:* Stopping initiation to prevent rejection; preemptive apologies; claiming to "prefer being alone."
    3. *Social Transparency:* Packing books ten seconds faster at the bell to exit first; vocal volume dropping on the final syllable; stopping all phrasing that requires someone else to say yes (*"Wait up"*, *"Can I sit here?"*).
